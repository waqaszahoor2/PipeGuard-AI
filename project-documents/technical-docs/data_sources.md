# Data Sources and Attribution

The bundled dataset ZIP contains its original readme, source catalog, licences,
citations, provenance metadata and integrity hashes.

Primary modules:

- BattLeDIM L-Town benchmark, Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.4017659, CC BY 4.0.
- City of Calgary Public Water Main, Water Main Breaks and Community District
  Boundaries under the City of Calgary open-data terms.

Review the original `LICENSES_AND_CITATION.md` inside the ZIP before redistribution
or publication.
