# Scientific Boundaries

PipeGuard AI is an early-warning and inspection-support prototype.

It can support:

- detection of unusual hydraulic patterns;
- ranking of suspected research zones;
- review of recorded public asset attributes;
- organization of inspection findings.

It cannot independently establish:

- a confirmed physical leak;
- exact corrosion or wall thickness;
- remaining useful life;
- internal cracks;
- maximum hydraulic capacity;
- a repair decision.

A field technician and appropriate inspection method are always required.
