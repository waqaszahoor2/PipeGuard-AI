# PipeGuard AI — Scientific Boundaries & Limitations

PipeGuard AI is developed to demonstrate modern full-stack web engineering, hydro-dynamic telemetry visualization, and responsible machine-learning evaluation.

---

## 🚫 What PipeGuard AI CANNOT Do

1. **Confirm Physical Leakage**: The application processes hydro-dynamic pressure and flow rate variance heuristics. It cannot physically guarantee that water is escaping a pipe wall without field acoustic/CCTV inspection.
2. **Determine Corrosion Depth or Wall Thickness**: Physical pipe wall loss requires specialized ultrasonic or electromagnetic inline inspection (ILI) tools.
3. **Predict Exact Structural Lifespan**: Remaining pipe lifespan depends on soil acidity, frost depth, traffic loading, and water chemistry beyond telemetry scope.
4. **Locate Micro-Leaks**: Low-volume background leaks (< 0.1 L/s) fall below nominal hydraulic sensor resolution.

---

## 🔒 Responsible Data & Ethical AI Commitment

- **No False Certainty**: The platform explicitly labels all risk scores as research indices and displays prominent disclaimers across all calculation tools.
- **Technician-in-the-Loop**: All administrative work orders require human technician verification before physical intervention.
- **Open Benchmark Reproducibility**: All evaluation metrics are published transparently on the Model Information page.
